/ The problem

The missing boundary

AI agents operate across changing state. When something goes wrong, the original context disappears.

01

World changes

Between failure and investigation, state drifts. The world that caused the bug is gone.

02

No snapshot

Logs and traces don't preserve the exact world state for re-running the agent.

03

Guesswork

Without reproduction, you can't prove your fix works. Debugging blind.
